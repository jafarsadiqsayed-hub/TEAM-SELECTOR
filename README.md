# 🎭 College Arts Fest – Team Selection Portal

A turn-based team selection web application designed for live college stage events, projector presentations, and multi-device drafting.

---

## 🚀 How to Run the Portal (Super Simple!)

You **do not need any programming experience or extra software installed**.

### Method 1: Double-Click the Launcher (Windows)
Double-click the **`START_PORTAL.bat`** file located in this folder. It will immediately open the portal in your default web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, etc.).

### Method 2: Open `index.html` Directly
Right-click on **`index.html`** ➡️ Choose **Open with** ➡️ Select **Google Chrome** or **Microsoft Edge**.

---

## 🎯 Key Features & How to Use Them

### 1. Live Turn-Based Team Selection
- **Active Team Indicator**: The top status bar clearly indicates whose turn it is (e.g. **RED DRAGONS' TURN** or **BLUE PHOENIX' TURN**).
- **Drafting a Student**: Click on any available student card to select them for the active team.
- **Stage Hero Reveal**: When selected, a dramatic full-screen spotlight reveal showcases the student's photo, name, roll number, and section with confetti fireworks and audio fanfare!
- **Automatic Turn Switching**: The portal automatically alternates turns from Team A ➡️ Team B ➡️ Team A.
- **Locking**: Once drafted, a student card is marked and locked so they can never be selected twice.

### 2. Live Team Rosters
- **Left Panel (Team A)**: Shows Team A's drafted students with live count and section breakdown (Secondary, Senior Secondary, Degree and PG).
- **Right Panel (Team B)**: Shows Team B's drafted students with live count and section breakdown.

### 3. Stage & Projector Presentation Mode
- Click the **📺 Screen Icon** in the top bar or press the **`F`** key on your keyboard to toggle high-contrast **Fullscreen Presentation Mode** optimized for large stage projectors and auditorium displays.

### 4. Search & Filter
- **Instant Search**: Type any student name, Student ID, or section in the search bar.
- **Section Tabs**: Click **Secondary**, **Senior Secondary**, or **Degree & PG** to filter the pool instantly.

### 5. Undo Last Selection
- Click **↩️ Undo Pick** or press **`Ctrl + Z`** to revert the most recent pick.
- The student is immediately returned to the available pool, and the turn is restored to the team that made that pick.

### 6. Operator & Admin Control Center
Click the **⚙️ Admin Center** button in the header to:
- **Add / Edit / Delete Students**: Add real students (ID, Name, Section, Photo) and upload photos directly from your computer.
- **Customize Teams & Fest Branding**: Change the fest title, subtitle, Team A & Team B names, logos, and taglines.
- **Coin Toss**: Run a random coin flip mini-game on stage to determine which team gets the 1st pick.
- **Export Roster to Excel/CSV**: Download the final selected teams in an official spreadsheet for printing and event records.
- **Import Students from CSV**: Bulk load your student list with 1 click.
- **Reset Entire Selection**: Start a fresh draft anytime.

## 📱 Multi-Device & Projector Setup

- **Dual Screen / Projector**: Open one browser window on your main laptop screen and drag a second window to the projector in Presentation Mode (`F`). Any student picked on the laptop will instantly update on the projector screen in real time!
- **Sound Effects**: Audio fanfare and chimes are generated natively in the browser — no internet or sound files required.
